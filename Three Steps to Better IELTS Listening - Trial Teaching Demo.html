Learner Profile & Pedagogical Objective

Diagnostic insight and targeted listening strategy for aspiring international students.

Target Score 6.0 – 6.5
Core Learning Challenge:
Key Bottleneck: Students tend to listen word-by-word and choose answers based on familiar keywords without anticipating grammar or filtering negative qualifiers.
Instructional Solution

The 3-Step Scaffolding Framework

1. Predict: Reduce real-time cognitive load by pre-identifying word class (adverb vs noun) and semantic constraints.
2. Listen: Move away from surface-level word matching toward key information filtering and paraphrase tracking.
3. Check: Validate grammatical correctness, singular/plural agreement, and negative distractor traps.
💡 Lesson Goal: Empower students to listen selectively for meaning rather than getting lost in every word.

Step 1: Predict the Answer Type

Look at the words around each gap to anticipate the grammatical category.

1. The speaker should dress ______. an adverb
2. She may borrow a ______ from her mother. a noun
3. She does not need to bring her exam ______. a noun
4. She needs to bring her ______. a document

💡 Grammar clues to look for:

dress + adverb
borrow a + noun
exam + noun
my / her + noun
