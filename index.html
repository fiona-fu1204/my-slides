Find the Mystery Person or Thing!

Restrictive Relative Clauses: who, which and that as subjects

🏫 高一英语 (Senior 1)
⏱️ 15-Minute Demo Lesson
🎯 核心目标：句子合并与精准指认

"Today, we are going to use one sentence to describe a person or a thing."

今天，我们学习如何用一个句子，精准描述并锁定神秘人物或物品！
🕵️ Task 1: Mystery Guess
Read the clues and find the mystery person & thing | 读线索，猜猜他们是谁/是什么
Step 1: Observation
Class Instructions: 1. Think for 10s  |  2. Talk with your partner  |  3. Make a guess! 先独立思考 ➔ 同桌轻声交流 ➔ 说出猜测
⏳ 10s Thinking
👤 Clue A: Mystery Person
  • • It is a person. 这是一个人物。
  • • It is someone who likes music. 这是某个喜欢音乐的人。
  • • It is someone who can speak English well. 这是某个英语讲得很好的人。
❓ Who is this? (A singer? An English teacher? A classmate?)
📦 Clue B: Mystery Thing
  • • It is a thing. 这是一个物品。
  • • It is something that is useful. 这是某种很有用的东西。
  • • It is something which many students use every day. 这是许多学生每天都在使用的东西。
❓ What is this? (A smartphone? A dictionary? A pen?)
👀 Notice & Discover
Compare the simple sentences and the combined sentence | 仔细观察两组句子
Sentence Pair 1:
I have a friend. + He likes English.
➔ I have a friend who / that likes English.
Sentence Pair 2:
This is a phone. + It takes clear photos.
➔ This is a phone which / that takes clear photos.
🤔 Guiding Questions (思考与归纳):
Q1: What does "who/that likes English" describe? 它修饰前面的哪个人？
➔ It describes the friend!
Q2: Is "friend" a person or a thing?
➔ A person (人) ➔ use who / that
Q3: Is "phone" a person or a thing?
➔ A thing (物) ➔ use which / that
Q4: What words do "who" and "which" replace?
➔ They replace He and It!
✨ Takeaway: 定语从句紧跟在被修饰的名词后，用来修饰限定它！
📐 The Golden Rules: Summary
How to connect sentences | 核心规则与极简结构图
Step 3: Generalization
👤 Person (人)  ➔  who / that
被修饰的名词是指人时，关系代词用 who 或 that
🧑‍🏫
📦 Thing (物)  ➔  which / that
被修饰的名词是指物品时，关系代词用 which 或 that
📚
Replacement Function (替代功能): The relative pronoun replaces he / she / it / they. 关系代词替代简单句中的人称代词，并把两个句子平滑连接。
Sentence Pattern 结构图
Noun (名词) + who / which / that + Verb (动词)
• a student + who/that + likes basketball
• a book + which/that + is interesting
* 先行词 Antecedent：前面被修饰的名词（认准是“人”还是“物”）

🛠️ Task 2: Group Work - Sentence Repair Shop
Collaborate in groups of 3-4 | 句子修理站：四人分工协作合并句子
Group Work (3.5 Mins)
1. Matcher: 找出先行词名词
2. Connector: 选 who / which / that
3. Writer: 写出完整句子
4. Reporter: 代表全组汇报展示
Sentence 1:
I know a boy. He plays football well.
支架：I know a boy plays football well.
Sentence 2:
This is a book. It is interesting.
支架：This is a book is interesting.
Sentence 3:
We have a teacher. She is very patient.
支架：We have a teacher is very patient.
✅ Reference Answers:
1. I know a boy who / that plays football well.
2. This is a book which / that is interesting.
3. We have a teacher who / that is very patient.

Common mistakes analysis | 观察三句话：判断对错并说出原因
1. I know a boy which plays football well. ❌
➔ 诊断：boy 是人，不能使用 which 指代！
Misuse of which
2. This is a book who is interesting. ❌
➔ 诊断：book 是物品，不能使用 who 指代！
Misuse of who
3. I know a boy who / that plays football well. ✅
➔ 诊断：完全正确！先行词是人，选用 who 或 that。

📦 Summary & Homework
Wrap up and consolidation | 总结与分层任务
Wrap-up (1 Min)
🎓 Lesson Summary in 3 Sentences
1. A relative clause describes a noun. 定语从句用来详细描述前面的名词。
2. Person ➔ who / that  |  Thing ➔ which / that 指人配 who/that，指物配 which/that。
3. The pronoun replaces he, she, or it. 关系代词替代了原代词，不要重复写 he/she/it。
🎉 Great job! Mission Accomplished!
